Elite Volleyball Academy



Elite Volleyball Academy is a junior volleyball club in Delta Region of USA Volleyball. EVA's mission is to provide highly positive, competitive, and effort based team culture. Our coaching principles empower and develop competitive, confident players who thrive not only as volleyball players but as young individuals . Our vision is to create a nationally respected program that is known to develop our athletes in skill, game knowledge, and team principles/culture. Our goal each season is to provide each player, family, and the team with a meaningful and enriching experience while teaching the game, team concepts, skills and many lessons that transfer into life. We are here to serve all highly driven and motivated players who seek serious level of training to provide them with development in volleyball skill and game knowledge as well as help each player achieve big personal and team goals. We strive to compete at the highest level in all age groups. We offer highly knowledgeable coaching staff lead by club director Tanja Eckart. We are passionate about team-first concepts and teach and live "We vs. Me" team culture. Within this culture we teach players how being the best you can be individually is making your team the best that it can be - and that's the ultimate goal of any team sport!
